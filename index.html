Our story

Started with a counter and a coffee grinder

Ember & Bean began as a small corner counter serving coffee to students on their way to class. It grew into a full kitchen, but the idea never changed: cook like you know who's eating it. We shop fresh most mornings, keep the kitchen open late, and leave the couch by the window free for whoever needs it most.

Today we're a spot for study sessions, first dates, family dinners, and the after-work crowd — all under one warm roof in the heart of Cumilla.

Why people keep coming back

  • Fresh ingredients

    We shop for produce most mornings and skip anything that's been sitting around. If it's not fresh, it's not on the plate.

  • Made to order

    Nothing is pre-plated under a heat lamp. Your order goes into the kitchen the moment you place it.

  • Cozy atmosphere

    Warm light, soft seating, and enough room to actually talk. Come for the food, stay for the couch by the window.

  • Fast service

    Most orders reach your table in under 20 minutes, even during the evening rush.
